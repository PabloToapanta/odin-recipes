Homemade pizza

Main Page

This is a great recipe when you don't want to wait for the dough to rise. You just mix it and allow it to rest for 5 minutes and then it's ready to go! It yields a soft, chewy crust.

If you're looking for a homemade pizza crust recipe that's great for beginners, you're in luck. This top-rated recipe is super easy to throw together on a whim – and it puts the store-bought stuff to shame. Learn how to make the best pizza crust of your life with just a few ingredients, find out how to shape the dough, and get our best storage secrets.

Ingredients

  • Yeast
  • Sugar
  • Bread Flour
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Let the Dough Come to Room Temperature

    After you're finished mixing the ingredients, allow your dough to rest on the counter for about 5 minutes. This allows the gluten to relax, making the dough much easier to stretch and shape.

  2. Prepare Your Surface With Oil

    Instead of using a large amount of flour (which can make the crust tough), rub your work surface and hands with a few tablespoons of olive oil and a light dusting of flour if needed. This prevents sticking, encourages a crispy texture, and helps create a beautiful golden color.

  3. Shape the Dough

    Stretch the dough in the air, use a rolling pin, or gently pat it out with your hands. Avoid overworking the dough, as this can make it tough. Shape it into an even circle about 10 to 12 inches wide and roughly ⅓-inch thick. If it’s too thin, it may not properly support the sauce and toppings.
